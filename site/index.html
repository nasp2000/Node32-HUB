One firmware family. One pack per device.

Every pack is the same ESP32 codebase compiled into a dedicated device: a camera that also records, a timing terminal that also emails, a radio that also runs alarms. A pack carries only the functions that device needs — you flash it, configure it on its own web page, and it runs on your network with no cloud, no account and no app.

How to choose

  1. Which board do you have? Most packs run on an ESP32-S3 N16R8. GcodeSender needs an ESP32-P4.
  2. What does it have to do? A pack bundles the functions that device needs: capture and record, time and notify, play and schedule, send and resume.
  3. What does it talk to? The pack page lists the bus, the protocol and the connector.
