Every setting

What each field on the camera page does, the values it accepts and what happens when you leave it alone. Five tabs: Cam, Pic, Rec, Vis (P4 only) and Mot.

1. Capture

Frame size, compression and exposure on the Cam tab.

FieldValuesDefaultWhat it does
Resolution320×240 to 1920×1080, plus 2048×1536 and 2592×1944800×600Frame size. Options above the sensor's maximum are disabled; larger frames cost PSRAM and drop the frame rate.
Quality4 to 6320JPEG quality — lower is better and bigger.
FPS1 to 3012Frames per second. The maximum depends on the frame size and the sensor, and is clamped when you exceed it.
Gain ceiling2× to 128×—How far the sensor may raise gain in low light. High values brighten the image and add noise.
Special effectNone · Negative · Grayscale · Red · Green · Blue · SepiaNoneColour effect applied in the sensor.
White balanceAuto · Sunny · Cloudy · Office · HomeAutoFixed white balance presets; Auto follows the scene.

2. Picture

Brightness, contrast and the manual exposure controls on the Pic tab.

FieldValuesDefaultWhat it does
Brightness / Contrast / Saturation−2 to +20Sensor-side adjustments, applied before compression.
AE level−2 to +20Exposure bias when auto exposure is on.
Manual gain0 to 30—Only used when AGC is off; shown as 1× to 31×.
Manual exposure0 to 1200—Only used when AEC is off.
Mirror / Flip / AWB / AEC / AGC / Lens correctionon · offAWB, AEC, AGC onPer-block switches. Turning AGC or AEC off hands control to the manual fields above.

3. Recording

The Rec tab holds everything that writes to the SD card.

FieldValuesDefaultWhat it does
DashCam1 to 30 min, 0 = offoffRotates the recording every N minutes so the card never fills with one file.
Timelapse0 to 600 s, 0 = offoffOne frame every N seconds, written into a 10 fps AVI.
Boot modeOff · Photo · Video · Motion · TimelapseOffWhat the camera starts doing by itself after a power cycle.
Codec (P4 only)MJPEG (.avi) · H.264 (.264)MJPEGHidden on the S3. H.264 records smaller files but the frame size and rate are capped.
H.264 bitrate1 to 8 Mbps4Only when H.264 is selected; the estimate line shows the cost in MB per minute.
H.264 containerRaw (.264) · AVI (.avi)RawAVI is easier to open, raw is what most editors want for the encoder output.

4. Motion

The Mot tab: a grayscale difference on a 40×30 grid, with the action being record or photo.

FieldValuesDefaultWhat it does
Motion detectionon · offoffMaster switch for the detector.
Sensitivity1 to 100—How much change counts as movement. High values trigger on noise.
Confirm frames1 to 20—How many consecutive frames must show change before it triggers.
Stop frames2 to 30—How many quiet frames end the recording.
Post-record0 to 30 s—Keeps recording after the movement stops.
Cooldown0 to 120 s, 0 = offoffQuiet period after a trigger, so one event does not become ten files.
