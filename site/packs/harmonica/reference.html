Every setting

The player has one screen and a handful of panels. This page lists what each control does.

1. Transport

ControlWhat it does
Play / PauseStarts or stops the current item; the label follows the state.
Previous / Stop / NextMoves through the current list: stations, recent, favourites, SD library or UPnP.
VolumeSlider and ± buttons; the change is applied immediately and survives a reboot.
SeekSD files only — live streams cannot be scrubbed.
Shuffle / RepeatRepeat one or repeat all; shuffle applies to the active list.
SleepStops playback after the chosen time.
EqualiserGain bands applied to the decoded audio.

2. Where the audio comes from

SourceWhat it does
Stream URLPlay any MP3/AAC stream URL you paste in.
DiscoverStation search by name, genre and country, with paging.
SD libraryMP3 files in /library, browsable by folder with search; queue ordered or random.
PSRAMFallback when there is no card; contents are lost on reboot.
FavouritesSave the current station and play it with one tap.
PlaylistsNamed lists saved on the SD as .m3u, so they open in other players too.
UPnP / DLNABrowse a media server on the network and queue from it.
ResumeOn boot, either nothing, the last item, or the last playlist.

3. Alarms

The agenda plays a station at a time of day, with a volume ramp instead of a jump scare.

FieldValuesWhat it does
Daysone or more weekdaysWhich days the slot fires.
TimeHH:MMStart of the slot.
Stationany saved stationWhat plays.
Enabledon · offKeeps the slot without deleting it.

4. Hardware

ItemDetail
BoardESP32-S3 N16R8 — PSRAM buffers the stream.
OutputI2S to a UDA1334A DAC, line out to an amplifier.
StoragemicroSD for the library and the playlists.
NetworkWi-Fi, or Ethernet on boards that have it.
