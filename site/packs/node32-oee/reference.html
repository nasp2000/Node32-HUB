Every setting

The board turns eight inputs into production counts and OEE. This page lists each field, its range and what it changes.

1. Channels

Each of the eight channels is configured independently on the Config tab.

FieldValuesWhat it does
Nametext, up to 31 charactersLabel shown in the tables and in the emails.
ModeCounter · Presence · SpeedCounter counts pulses; Presence reports a state (running/stopped); Speed reports a rate.
GPIO IN / IN2 / OUTpin numberHidden on the 8DI8DO, where the pins are fixed. Anything you change needs a reboot.
Debounce0 to 65535 msIgnores pulses shorter than this. 20 to 100 ms covers most sensors.
Goal0 or more, 0 = unlimitedAfter N pulses the relay stops firing until Reset.
DO delay / duration0 to 65535 msDelay between the pulse and the relay, and how long the relay stays on. Zero duration means no output.
Multiplier / Divider1 to 9999 eachScales counts into parts: parts = counts × multiplier ÷ divider. Remainders carry over so slow rates never lose counts.
Only GPIO INon · offPassive mode: disables the relay and the goal for that channel.
Gateany input, or noneThe presence channel that gates availability in OEE.
Scrapany input, or nonePulses here are subtracted from quality, and never reset the inactivity timer.
Relay DOnone or DO1 to DO8Which relay a channel drives (assignments are named separately).

2. OEE

Availability, performance and quality are computed over the shift window, not from a spreadsheet.

FieldValuesWhat it does
Shift windowstart and end timeThe period the numbers refer to; day-aligned or custom.
Targetparts per shiftGoal line drawn on the dashboard and used by the goal-vs-actual panel.
AvailabilitycomputedPlanned time minus stops, gated by the presence channel if one is assigned.
PerformancecomputedActual count against the ideal rate for the window.
QualitycomputedProduced minus scrap.

3. The tabs

TabShows
StatusPer-channel mode, counts, cycle, quantity per minute, state and last event.
ConfigEverything in section 1, plus the relay naming and the global options.
ShiftsShift definitions and targets.
ShiftdayWhich shift applies to which day.
OEEA×P×Q for the current window, the per-hour bars and the goal line.

4. Alerts and history

ItemWhat it does
Inactivity alertEmails when a machine stops producing for longer than the configured time.
CSV historyOne file per day on the SD, served from the Files page.
Shift reportThe same numbers as the CSV, sent by email at the end of the window.
Daily rebootOptional; keeps a long-running cabinet fresh without anyone on site.

5. If it does not work

SymptomCheck
Counts doubleDebounce too short for a bouncing contact, or the multiplier set above 1.
No counts at allThe sensor's supply and polarity against the isolated input, and the channel mode.
OEE shows 0%Window with no production, or the gate channel never reported presence.
No emailThe SMTP account on /email; the board is Ethernet-only, so check the cable and the lease.
CSV missingThe card and its free space; a full card stops the daily file.
