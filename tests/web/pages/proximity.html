⏱️ Shift Management

The system automatically detects shift transitions and writes a CSV on the SD.

🛑 Planned stops

Daily planned stops (e.g. lunch break) excluded from Availability — planned busy time (ISO 22400-2). Unused rows stay off: an empty list keeps the current behavior.

⌨️ SHIFTDAY Command

💡 Use this command on the Scheduled Commands pages to send the shift CSV by email.
📅 SHIFTDAY — Sends current day's shift CSV by email
Example: shiftday
📅 SHIFTDAY [date] — Sends shift CSV for a specific date
Example: shiftday 28-03-2026
💡 Separate multiple emails with commas. These recipients are added to the global recipients configured in the Email section.
